Cross-condition SOH estimator comparison

No algorithm generalizes across datasets — yet.

Four SOH estimators were compared on identical features across four working-condition datasets. The best, SVR, reaches 0.197 MAE leave-one-dataset-out — but none clears the acceptance bars. The gap between within-domain and cross-domain accuracy is the story.
